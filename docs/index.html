Jewish Population Timeline in Europe (1933–1945)

A historical, geocoded visualization of Jewish population estimates at cities, ghettos, deportation hubs, and extermination sites across Europe.

Period

1933–1945

Milestones

1933, 1935, 1937, 1938, 1939, 1940, 1941, 1942, 1943, 1944, 1945

Coverage

Europe-wide cities, ghettos, hubs, and camps

Source Note

Secondary educational estimates for spatial visualization.

Open interactive map Open GIF animation Open MP4 animation

Visualization notes

  • Height reflects local Jewish population size.
  • Color ramps from blue to yellow/orange to dark red.
  • Deportation hubs and extermination sites are shown on the ground plane.
